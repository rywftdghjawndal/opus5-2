THE MECHANICS OF A SECOND
This wheel decides what a second is.
A hairspring pulls it home.
every swing takes the same time — isochronism
A fork rocks in step —
and frees the gear: one tooth at a time.
The tick.
The push comes from a coiled spring — two days of patience.
Gears trade its strength for speed.
Five ticks — one second.
300 ticks — one minute.
Assemble it all —
and the second is finished.
the oscillator — still beating.
A second isn’t found.
It’s made.
18,000 beats to the hour.
